AWS Guidelines for the use of AI

The rapid development of AI is changing the way humans learn and research mathematics. The core mission of the Arizona Winter School (AWS) remains unchanged: to help students understand mathematics, learn concepts and techniques underpinning recent advances, and build connections within the mathematical community.

The AWS emphasizes collaborative learning. Students learn not only from lectures, but also by discussing mathematics, explaining ideas to one another, and working through difficulties together.

The AWS is not an AI mathathon. The emphasis is on mathematical understanding rather than mathematical output. Students are neither expected nor required to produce original mathematical results using AI.

We encourage students to follow these principles:

  • Prioritize understanding. Focus on understanding mathematical ideas and developing the ability to explain them independently.
  • Engage with people, not just AI. Ask questions directly to lecturers and discuss mathematics with fellow students, rather than relying primarily on AI.
  • Participate actively. We strongly recommend you refrain from using electronic devices like laptops or tablet during lectures, to encourage attention and interaction.

The success of AWS should be measured by what students learn together, the mathematical ideas they exchange, and the relationships they build.

As AI technology and its role in mathematics continue to evolve rapidly, we will closely monitor these developments and update these guidelines as appropriate.
